# Canonical Norn product language

## Norn Profile

The persistent evidence-backed record of skills, projects, ecosystems, constraints and proof. Every capability is marked `verified`, `claimed` or `aspirational`.

## Norn Feed

The normalised opportunity pipeline. Every item carries a type, issuer, value, deadline, requirements, source URL, source timestamp and source classification.

## Norn Score

An explainable 0–100 assessment composed of eligibility, technical fit, strategic alignment, evidence strength, reward value, preparation cost, deadline pressure, competitive intensity and long-term value.

## Norn Gaps

A requirement-by-requirement evidence matrix with four statuses: `verified`, `partial`, `missing` and `not_applicable`.

## Norn Pack

A draft application workspace generated from verified facts. It includes the opportunity summary, verified evidence, requirement matrix, completion plan and evidence index. It excludes unsupported claims and requires human approval.

## Norn Watch

The follow-through layer for deadlines, evidence work, readiness, submission receipts and outcomes.

## Positioning
