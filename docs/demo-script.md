# 90-second demo script

## 0–10 seconds — Problem

“Builders do not lack opportunity links. They lack a trustworthy way to know which opportunity is worth pursuing and what proof is still missing.”

Show the Norn landing view and six product surfaces.

## 10–25 seconds — Norn Profile and Feed

“This is Norn Profile. Skills and projects are separated into verified, claimed and aspirational states. Norn Feed normalises grants, hackathons, bounties and partnerships with deadlines, value, requirements and source provenance.”

Open Profile, then return to Feed.

## 25–45 seconds — Norn Score

“Select an opportunity and Norn produces an explainable fit score—not keyword overlap. It weighs eligibility, technical fit, strategic value, evidence strength, reward, effort, deadline pressure and long-term value.”

Open the OKX.AI Genesis item and show Score anatomy.

## 45–60 seconds — Norn Gaps

“Norn Gaps shows the crucial answer: are we actually ready? Here, marketplace activation, public demo and submission proof are still missing. Norn recommends closing those gaps instead of submitting weak claims.”

Open Gaps.

## 60–75 seconds — Norn Pack and Watch

“Norn Pack prepares application material using verified facts only and records every excluded claim. Nothing is submitted without approval. Norn Watch then tracks the deadline, next action and result.”

Generate the Pack, then show Watch.

## 75–90 seconds — ASP proof

“The same workflow is exposed as a pay-per-call OKX.AI service. Another agent sends a capability profile and opportunity set, receives an HTTP 402 challenge, pays on X Layer and gets a ranked opportunity brief with request and result digests.”

Show the unpaid 402 response, then the paid response and provenance fields. End on: **Norn — Find where your proof has value.**
